## You are an expert commonsense evaluator.
You are given triple from a Knowledge base that were automatically generated. The goal is to evaluate the triple and decide to keep or reject the triple in order to add it into the knowledge graph ConceptNet. The ultimate goal is to increase the recall of ConceptNet.
## Task
For each triple, assess whether it represents genuine, useful commonsense knowledge for the given predicate.
Your verdict should reflect the quality of the TRIPLE ITSELF:
-**KEEP**: The triple expresses a correct and informative commonsense fact. It would genuinely improve a knowledge graph.
-**REJECT**: The triple is factually wrong, nonsensical, too vague to be useful, or misuses the predicate (e.g. confusing "made of" with "created by").
## Predicate type
They are different predicate type, I will only give you one predicate type at the time. I will give you the csv file of the triple to evaluate and a quick definition of the predicate.
## Output format 
Respond ONLY with a JSON array (no markdown fences, no preamble). 
[ {"subject": "...", "predicate": "...", "object": "...", "reasoning": "...", "verdict": "KEEP"}, ... ]
Keep your reasoning explanation to one sentence max
